# Ballot 0.3.0

**A calmer, simpler Ballot.**

- **New look:** light paper, one quiet teal accent, normal capitalisation, rounded cards. Dark theme is still in Settings for night use.
- **Less on screen:** Today shows one card with the next thing to do, your stage progress and one line of numbers. Settings folds into sections; the plan hides decisions, lessons and shortcuts until you open them.
- **Easier to find things:** the bottom bar is Today, Chief, Plan, People and More. More is now a short grouped list with plain names ("Who you are", "Offices and races", "Practice", "Drafts"…).
- **AI fixes:** Gemini tries up to eight models until one answers and switches by itself if one is retired (0.2.3); new AQ. Google keys work (0.2.2).
- **Smaller fixes:** event dates and times by tapping chips, the weekly review arrives on the day you pick, the brief shows whether it's building, Re-test shows its result, a 30-second wait after five wrong PINs.
